# Privacy Policy — Water Sort Game

**Last Updated:** October 17, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Water Sort Game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about the information processed by our game and its third-party services.

By using Water Sort Game, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

Water Sort Game does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, or phone number to play.

The game may store the following information locally on your device:

- Game progress and completed levels.
- In-game coins and rewards.
- Game settings and preferences.

This information is used to provide the core gameplay experience. Locally stored data may be lost if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Google AdMob and its partners may collect or process certain information, such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information, depending on your device settings, consent choices, and the services enabled.

This information may be used for advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

For more information, please review the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 4. In-App Purchases

The game may offer optional in-app purchases, such as coin packages, an ad-removal option, and VIP subscriptions.

Purchases are processed through Google Play. We do not directly collect or store your payment card details.

Google Play may process purchase information to complete transactions, manage subscriptions, and prevent fraud. Such processing is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device where supported by the app.

We do not operate a dedicated game account system or game-progress server as part of the standard gameplay experience.

However, third-party services integrated into the app may process information on their own systems. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of those providers.

We do not sell users' personal information.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Locally stored gameplay information generally remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
