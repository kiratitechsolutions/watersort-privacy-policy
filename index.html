Welcome to Water Sort Game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile game. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about information processed by the app and its third-party services.

Water Sort Game does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, or phone number to play.

We do not sell users' personal information. We do not directly share locally stored gameplay progress with other users.

Locally stored gameplay information generally remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
